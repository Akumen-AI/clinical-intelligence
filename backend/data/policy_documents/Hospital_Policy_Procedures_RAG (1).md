# **1\. Purpose and Scope**

This document establishes the official policies and standard operating procedures governing clinical and administrative operations at Metropolitan General Hospital. It serves as the primary knowledge base for the hospital's AI-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot system, ensuring consistent, accurate, and policy-compliant responses to staff queries.

## **1.1 Purpose**

**•** Define standard protocols for patient care, administrative functions, and emergency procedures.

**•** Provide a structured, machine-readable knowledge base for RAG chatbot ingestion and retrieval.

**•** Ensure regulatory compliance with HIPAA, Joint Commission, and CMS standards.

**•** Promote patient safety, staff accountability, and operational excellence.

## **1.2 Scope**

This policy applies to all hospital personnel including:

**•** Medical staff (physicians, nurses, allied health professionals)

**•** Administrative and clerical staff

**•** Support services (housekeeping, food services, maintenance)

**•** Contract workers, students, and volunteers operating within hospital premises

# **2\. Patient Admission Procedures**

## **2.1 Emergency Admissions**

**1\.** Patient arrives at Emergency Department (ED); triage nurse assigns ESI level within 5 minutes.

**2\.** Vital signs recorded immediately; attending physician notified for ESI Level 1–2.

**3\.** Registration staff collects patient demographics and insurance information within 30 minutes of arrival.

**4\.** HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices provided; patient or authorized representative signs acknowledgment.

**5\.** Electronic Health Record (EHR) encounter created; medical record number assigned.

**6\.** Primary care physician or specialist notified per on-call schedule if inpatient admission required.

## **2.2 Elective / Scheduled Admissions**

**•** Pre-admission testing (PAT) completed 1–7 days prior to procedure date.

**•** Patient reports to Admissions Desk; insurance pre-authorization confirmed before procedure.

**•** Consent forms signed and witnessed; copies provided to patient and scanned into EHR.

**•** Nursing staff completes admission assessment within 2 hours of bed assignment.

**•** Advance directives reviewed and documented in EHR under patient safety flags.

## **2.3 Bed Management**

The Bed Management Office coordinates all inpatient placements and operates 24/7. Nursing supervisors receive bed alerts via the Capacity Management System (CMS). Target metrics:

| **Metric**           | **Target Standard** | **Escalation Threshold** |
| -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ED to Inpatient Bed  | ≤ 60 minutes        | \> 90 minutes            |
| PACU to ICU Transfer | ≤ 30 minutes        | \> 45 minutes            |
| Discharge Processing | ≤ 2 hours           | \> 3 hours               |
| Bed Turnaround (EVS) | ≤ 30 minutes        | \> 45 minutes            |

# **3\. Clinical Care Standards**

## **3.1 Medication Administration (Five Rights)**

All nursing staff must verify the Five Rights prior to every medication administration:

**•** Right Patient — verify using two identifiers (name + date of birth or MRN)

**•** Right Medication — confirm drug name, concentration, and form against MAR

**•** Right Dose — cross-check calculated dose with pharmacy order

**•** Right Route — confirm route matches physician order and patient capability

**•** Right Time — administer within the ±30-minute window for scheduled medications

**High-alert medications (anticoagulants, insulins, concentrated electrolytes) require independent double-check by a second licensed nurse before administration.**

## **3.2 Fall Prevention Protocol**

**1\.** Fall risk assessment performed on admission using the Morse Fall Scale; reassessed every 8 hours.

**2\.** Patients scoring ≥ 45 receive a yellow fall risk wristband and a FALL RISK door sign.

**3\.** Non-slip footwear and bed in lowest position maintained at all times for high-risk patients.

**4\.** Call light within reach; patient and family educated on fall prevention measures.

**5\.** Hourly rounding documented in EHR by assigned nurse.

## **3.3 Infection Control and Hand Hygiene**

**•** WHO 5 Moments for Hand Hygiene mandatory for all clinical personnel.

**•** Alcohol-based hand rub (ABHR) used unless hands visibly soiled (soap and water required).

**•** Contact, droplet, and airborne precautions implemented as ordered; PPE available at room entry.

**•** N95 respirators required for suspected or confirmed aerosolizing procedures (e.g., intubation, bronchoscopy).

**•** C. difficile cases: soap and water only; ABHR is ineffective against C. diff spores.

## **3.4 Pressure Injury Prevention**

Braden Scale assessed on admission and every 72 hours thereafter. Patients scoring ≤ 18:

**•** Repositioned minimum every 2 hours; documented in EHR.

**•** Pressure-relieving mattress ordered via Central Supply.

**•** Skin inspection head-to-toe at each shift change; any Stage 2+ wounds photographed and documented.

**•** Wound Care nurse consulted for Stage 2 or higher pressure injuries.

# **4\. Patient Privacy and HIPAA Compliance**

## **4.1 Protected Health Information (PHI)**

All patient information — including name, date of birth, diagnosis, treatment records, financial data, and medical record numbers — constitutes Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA and must be handled in accordance with this policy.

## **4.2 Minimum Necessary Standard**

**•** Access PHI only to the extent necessary to perform assigned job duties.

**•** Do not access PHI for patients not under your direct care, including family members.

**•** EHR audit logs are reviewed monthly; unauthorized access results in disciplinary action up to termination.

## **4.3 Disclosure Rules**

| **Disclosure Scenario**         | **Authorization Required**  | **Notes**                 |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Treatment, Payment, Operations  | No                          | Minimum necessary applies |
| Patient request for own records | Yes — written               | Provide within 30 days    |
| Law enforcement subpoena        | No                          | Notify Privacy Officer    |
| Public health reporting         | No                          | Per state regulations     |
| Research                        | Yes — IRB waiver or consent | De-identify when possible |
| Media / marketing               | Yes — explicit written      | Never without consent     |

## **4.4 Breach Notification**

**1\.** Any suspected PHI breach reported to Privacy Officer within 1 hour of discovery.

**2\.** Privacy Officer conducts risk assessment within 24 hours.

**3\.** If breach confirmed, affected individuals notified within 60 calendar days.

**4\.** Breaches affecting > 500 individuals reported to HHS and local media simultaneously.

# **5\. Emergency Codes and Disaster Response**

## **5.1 Hospital Emergency Code Reference**

| **Code**             | **Meaning**                        | **Announce / Action**                                       |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Code Blue            | Cardiac / Respiratory Arrest       | Overhead PA + page Rapid Response Team                      |
| Code Red             | Fire                               | RACE: Rescue, Alarm, Contain, Extinguish/Evacuate           |
| Code Gray            | Combative Person / Security Threat | Security response + staff clear area                        |
| Code Pink            | Infant / Child Abduction           | Lock all exits; staff to assigned stations                  |
| Code Orange          | Mass Casualty / Disaster           | Activate Hospital Incident Command System (HICS)            |
| Code Black           | Bomb Threat                        | Do not touch suspicious objects; notify Security and Police |
| Code Silver          | Active Shooter / Weapon            | Run – Hide – Fight protocol; call 911                       |
| Code Triage Internal | Internal Disaster                  | Department heads report to Command Center                   |

## **5.2 Rapid Response Team (RRT) Activation**

Any staff member — including non-clinical — may activate the RRT when concerned about a patient's condition. RRT criteria include:

**•** Acute change in mental status

**•** Heart rate &lt; 40 or &gt; 130 bpm

**•** Respiratory rate &lt; 8 or &gt; 28 breaths/min

**•** SpO₂ < 90% on supplemental oxygen

**•** Systolic BP < 90 mmHg unresponsive to fluids

**•** Staff member 'worried' about a patient — no objective criteria needed

# **6\. Staff Responsibilities and Conduct**

## **6.1 General Conduct**

**•** Maintain professional demeanor with patients, visitors, and colleagues at all times.

**•** Arrive punctually; complete shift handoff using SBAR communication format.

**•** Wear hospital-issued ID badge visibly at or above waist level.

**•** Personal mobile phones restricted to break areas; clinical areas are device-free zones.

## **6.2 Mandatory Training Requirements**

| EHR System Training           | On hire + upgrades | Classroom / Virtual | Within 30 days of hire  |

## **6.3 Reporting Obligations**

Employees are obligated to report the following through the hospital's Safety Event Reporting System (SERS):

**•** Medication errors or near-misses

**•** Patient falls — with or without injury

**•** Equipment failures affecting patient safety

**•** Workplace injuries or exposures (blood, needlestick)

**•** Observed abuse, neglect, or exploitation of patients

**Retaliation against any employee who files a good-faith safety report is prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary action.**

# **7\. Data Governance for AI / RAG Chatbot System**

## **7.1 Document Classification**

All documents ingested into the RAG knowledge base must be classified by the Data Governance Committee as:

| **Classification** | **Examples**                                      | **Access Level**  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Public             | Visitor information, general FAQs                 | Unrestricted      |
| Internal           | Standard operating procedures, training materials | All staff         |
| Confidential       | HR records, financial reports, vendor contracts   | Role-based        |
| Restricted         | Patient PHI, legal counsel communications         | Need-to-know only |

## **7.2 Knowledge Base Update Protocol**

**1\.** Policy owners submit updated documents to the Knowledge Management team via the Document Management System (DMS).

**2\.** Content reviewed by the Policy Review Committee within 10 business days.

**3\.** Approved documents tagged with metadata: category, effective date, review date, owner.

**4\.** Documents ingested into vector database; embedding freshness confirmed within 24 hours.

**5\.** Retired policies flagged as 'superseded'; removed from active retrieval index.

## **7.3 Chatbot Response Quality & Compliance**

**•** All chatbot responses must cite source document and policy number where applicable.

**•** Clinical advice responses include disclaimer: 'This information is for reference only. Always follow your supervisor's instruction and clinical judgment.'

**•** Hallucination monitoring: weekly sampling of 50 random queries reviewed by Quality team.

**•** Out-of-scope queries (diagnosis, prescribing) escalated to appropriate clinical staff; chatbot does not generate clinical diagnoses.

**•** Chatbot logs retained for 7 years per document retention policy (MGH-POL-2023-009).

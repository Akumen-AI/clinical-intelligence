## Section 1: Purpose

This policy defines eligibility, accrual, and request procedures for paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and unpaid leave for all hospital staff, including clinical and non-clinical employees.

## Section 2: Employee Categories and Eligibility

2.1. Full-time employees (30+ hours/week) accrue PTO from date of hire.

2.2. Part-time employees (15–29 hours/week) accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis.

2.3. Per-diem and casual staff are not eligible for accrued PTO but retain statutory sick leave entitlements per local labor law.

## Section 3: PTO Accrual

3.1. Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per completed month of service (21 days annually).

3.2. Accrual increases to 2.08 days per month (25 days annually) after 5 years of continuous service.

3.3. Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following calendar year; balances above this cap are forfeited unless the employee is on approved medical leave at year-end.

## Section 4: Sick Leave

4.1. All employees accrue sick leave separately from PTO at a rate of 1 day per month, up to 12 days annually.

4.2. A physician's note is required for sick leave absences exceeding 3 consecutive scheduled shifts.

4.3. Clinical staff calling in sick for a shift must notify their department charge nurse or supervisor no later than 3 hours before shift start, except in genuine emergencies.

## Section 5: Requesting Leave

5.1. Non-emergency PTO requests must be submitted through the staff portal at least 14 days in advance for shifts, and 30 days in advance for leave exceeding 5 consecutive days.

5.2. Department heads must approve or deny a leave request within 5 business days of submission.

5.3. Leave requests during major holiday periods (as published annually by HR) are subject to department-specific blackout rules to maintain minimum safe staffing levels.

## Section 6: Unpaid Leave and Extended Absence

6.1. Employees may request unpaid leave of up to 90 days for personal reasons, subject to department head and HR approval.

6.2. Extended medical leave beyond accrued sick leave and PTO is governed separately under the hospital's Family and Medical Leave Policy (HR-POL-009).

6.3. Employees on unpaid leave exceeding 30 days do not accrue additional PTO or sick leave during that period.

## Section 7: Return to Work

7.1. Clinical staff returning from medical leave of 14 days or more must provide fitness-for-duty clearance from occupational health before resuming patient-facing duties.

7.2. Failure to return on the agreed return date without prior communication may be treated as voluntary resignation per Section 9 of the Employee Handbook.
